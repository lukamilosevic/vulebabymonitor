🌟 Core Architecture

Designed for Peaceful Nights

All functionality operates strictly within your local home network.

🛡️

100% Zero-Cloud Privacy

No cloud servers, no account logins, and no telemetry sent to the internet. Everything stays strictly locked inside your home Wi-Fi network with 0 mobile data usage.

🔋

Maximum Battery Savings

When baby is quiet (Silence-on-Wire), audio stream and hardware DAC shut down, keeping the phone processor in Deep Sleep (C-state). Streams only on real sound.

🎙️

VOX Audio & Smart 15s Hangover

Linear 16kHz PCM audio stream activates instantaneously on sound with calibrated VOX thresholds and a smart 15s hangover that auto-refreshes on baby movement.

📹

Video on Demand & PiP

Native Camera2 JPEG stream (~5 fps for zero heating), remote gentle night light, and floating Picture-in-Picture window that automatically reconnects on unlock.

🌙

Smart Nightstand (Lock Screen Video)

When sound is detected overnight, the parent phone automatically turns on and displays live video directly over the lock screen without unlocking.

🔐

Military Encryption & FHD Photo

ChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD + ECDH with 4-digit zero-typing PIN, two-way Talk-Back with echo suppression, and true sensor-resolution photos with NACK recovery.
