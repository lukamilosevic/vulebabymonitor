🛡️

100% Zero-Cloud Privacy

No cloud servers, no account logins, and no telemetry sent to the internet. Everything stays strictly locked inside your home Wi-Fi network.

⚡

Maximum Battery Life

When baby is sleeping quietly (Silence-on-Wire), the phone stops audio streaming and keeps the processor in deep sleep (Deep Sleep C-state). Streams only on sound.

🎙️

VOX Sound Detection

Instant audio streaming upon baby vocalization with calibrated thresholds and a smart 10s hangover extension, eliminating false alerts from white noise.

📹

Video on Demand & PiP

Native Camera2 hardware JPEG streaming without phone overheating, lens rotation, remote night light, and floating Picture-in-Picture window with auto-reconnect.

🔒

ECDH & ChaCha20 Encryption

Automatic key exchange and military-grade AEAD encryption with a 4-digit verification PIN protect your family from eavesdropping on shared Wi-Fi networks.

🌙

Smart Nightstand & Lock Screen

When sound is detected overnight, the parent phone automatically wakes up and displays live video directly over the lock screen without unlocking.

How It Works in 3 Steps

No complex configuration — works straight out of the box.

1

Install APK

Download and install BabyMonitor.apk on both Android phones.

2

Connect to Wi-Fi

Connect both phones to the same Wi-Fi router (or turn on Hotspot on one phone without mobile data).

3

Choose Roles

On one device tap 👶 Baby, and on the other tap 👪 Parent. Pairing is instant!
